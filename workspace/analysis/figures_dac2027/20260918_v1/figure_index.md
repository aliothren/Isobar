# Figure set v1: candidates for the paper

Run `20260918_v1`, generated by `scripts/plot_dac2027_v1.py` from the frozen
records. Nothing outside this directory was written. Every panel exists as
`panels/<name>.pdf` (vector), `.svg` (editable) and `preview/<name>.png`;
combined candidates are in `composites/`; the numbers behind each panel are
in `data/`.

Main configuration unless a panel says otherwise: 2.7B class model, 512-token
serial prefill, 544 of 576 dies on a 24x24 mesh with XY routing, NeuRRAM
derived row time 10.14 us, kappa = 2, 0.4 GB/s per link direction. Deltas are
written as `change = (value / reference - 1) x 100%`, so a negative number is
a reduction; the reference is named in every panel's data file.

Rebuild everything with:

    python3 scripts/plot_dac2027_v1.py --run-id 20260918_v1

Only the PDFs, the plotting data and this index are committed; the SVG
sources and the PNG previews are left out of the repository and come back
from the command above.

## Fig. 1  `fig1_phase_diagram`

**Supports:** Placement is a phase: where it decides the cost, at two levels.

**Caption draft.** Both axes are rates. Panel (a) is one die's two co-resident operators over all 70 enumerated macro seatings, under the per-operator overlap model, so its regions say whether communication is exposed or masked. Panel (b) is the whole-model serial prefill over the seven named seating rules, so its regions say which cost dominates. Reference points project published parameters onto this instance.

**Sources:** analysis/phase_diagram_isobar_k2.json, scripts/fig_phase_isobar.py

**Standalone panels / alternates:** `fig1a_intra_die_phase`, `fig1b_inter_die_phase`

## Fig. 2  `fig2_platform_workflow`

**Supports:** What the platform computes, and where the search sits in it.

**Caption draft.** Schematic. Mapping and partition generate the logical traffic; placement and routing turn it into link and port loads; latency and dynamic energy are two different aggregations of the same description. The search changes placement and partition and re-scores only the boundaries it touched. It optimises latency; energy is reported alongside. Carries no experimental numbers.

**Sources:** schematic

## Fig. 3  `fig3_external_validation`

**Supports:** What the cost model is checked against.

**Caption draft.** (a) Published placement measurements on a 4-chiplet package against the platform's two execution estimates; the round-serialized estimate tracks the measured ladder from below, with a residual of up to about 5%. The two estimates are model variants, not a confidence interval. (b) The same 13 candidate placements of a synthetic fan-out task scored by the analytic cost and by a cycle-level packet simulator, each relative to the strongest random layout; the identity line means equal relative change, not equal absolute time.

**Sources:** analysis/simba_t1.json, analysis/b_fanout_24x24_rerank.json

**Standalone panels / alternates:** `fig3a_silicon_placement`, `fig3b_packet_ranking`

## Fig. 4  `fig4_deployment_gain`

**Supports:** What the deployment choice is worth, and what it costs.

**Caption draft.** (a) Whole-model prefill latency of every evaluated layout, split into the non-communication cost and the hand-off cost. (b) The same layouts in latency and system dynamic energy: the compact rules buy energy with latency, while the search moves latency at no energy cost. (c) The same platform one level down, on the instance that has within-die freedom; that level carries no modelled energy.

**Sources:** analysis/mechanism_table.json, analysis/energy_export.json, analysis/intra_die_integration.json

**Standalone panels / alternates:** `fig4a_latency_breakdown`, `fig4b_latency_energy`, `fig4c_intra_die_layer`

## Fig. 5  `fig5_network_and_ablation`

**Supports:** The mechanism, on the real fabric.

**Caption draft.** One hand-off stage, the same 25 flows and 26.2 M words in every panel, drawn on the real 24x24 die grid with the evaluator's own routing. Colour is the service time each link direction owes at 0.4 GB/s, on one shared scale. A compact placement funnels the flows through few links, a dispersed one spreads them, and the search keeps the dispersion while removing the remaining stacking. The last panel replaces the objective and keeps everything else fixed.

**Sources:** scripts/search_eval.py (live), analysis/kappa_geometry_sweep.json, analysis/objective_ablation_k2.json

**Standalone panels / alternates:** `fig5_network_triptych`, `fig5_network_clustered`, `fig5_network_random_s3`, `fig5_network_sa_random_s1`, `fig5_network_least_manhattan`, `fig5d_objective_ablation`, `fig5_network_triptych_least_manhattan`

## Fig. 6  `fig6_conditions`

**Supports:** The conditions that decide how much placement is worth.

**Caption draft.** (a) One frozen pair of layouts re-scored across bandwidth, never re-searched: the communication improvement is constant and the whole-model gain follows the communication share. (b) Port capacity and package geometry, each cell against its own strongest evaluated baseline under one statistic. (c) Prefill and decode rank the same placements alike, which is why there is no separate decode search.

**Sources:** analysis/bw_slice_k2.json, analysis/kappa_geometry_sweep.json, analysis/main_config_results.json

**Standalone panels / alternates:** `fig6a_bandwidth_slice`, `fig6a_bandwidth_slice_with_share`, `fig6b_kappa_geometry`, `fig6b_kappa_geometry_12p5`, `fig6c_prefill_decode`

## Known gaps, stated rather than filled

- Fig. 3(a): the published T2 and T3 comparisons have recorded magnitude and shape disagreements and are not redrawn here; only T1 is. The older full comparison figure is unchanged in analysis/.
- Fig. 3(b): the packet simulator gives each node one terminal channel, so it cannot express kappa = 2; this panel is the kappa = 1 fan-out instance and is a communication batch, not the whole model.
- Fig. 4(c): the intra-die level has no energy coefficient on the macro link and no per-operator energy record for that instance, so its energy is not modelled, not zero.
- Fig. 5: one illustrative hand-off stage is drawn (ffn2 of layer 2), the same stage in every panel; the whole-model aggregate is in Fig. 4 and the mechanism table.
- Fig. 6(b): the two geometries are searched independently, so the same kappa can give slightly different endpoints; the cells are not forced to agree.
